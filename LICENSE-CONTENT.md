## What it does not cover

These keep their own terms:

- **Photographs** in `mheef-phase6/public/images/`. Each has its own
  author and licence (public domain, CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA), recorded with
  the image and shown beneath it on the site. CC BY-SA images must be
  shared under the same licence if adapted.
- **Place names** in `mheef-phase6/src/data/placeNames.json`, derived from
  [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/) (public domain).
- **Map imagery and terrain** shown on the globe (NASA Blue Marble,
  Natural Earth, Cesium ion and its providers), under their providers'
  terms.
- **Facts themselves.** Dates, figures and discoveries are not owned by
  anyone; the licence covers how they are written and compiled here. The
  cited sources remain the works of their authors.
- **Third-party software** (CesiumJS, Astro, React and other packages),
  under their own licences.
